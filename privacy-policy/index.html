Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Moola Flow app collects, how it is used and shared, and the choices you have. Moola Flow is developed and operated by James Njovu ("we", "us").

In short: we collect the account details and money records you enter so the app can work and sync across your devices. Google services we rely on (Firebase and AdMob) also collect some device and usage data. We do not sell your personal information. You can delete your account and all of your data at any time.

1. Information you provide

Account information

  • Email sign-up: your full name, email address and a password. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see or store your password.
  • Google sign-in: the name, email address and profile photo link from your Google account.
  • Profile: your display name, an optional profile picture, and your preferred currency and language.

Money records you enter

  • Transactions: amount, type (expense, income or transfer), date, category, account, description, tags, repeat schedule and an optional receipt photo.
  • Accounts: name, type (cash, bank, Mobile Money or card), opening balance, currency and an optional short label such as the last four digits of a card or account number. We never ask for full card numbers, PINs or banking passwords.
  • Categories and budgets: the names, colours and icons you choose, budget amounts, periods and alert settings.

Moola Flow is a record-keeping tool. It does not connect to your bank or Mobile Money provider, and it cannot move, hold or send money.

Purchases

If you subscribe to Premium, we store your Premium status, the subscription product and its expiry date. Payments are processed by Google Play. We never receive your card or payment details.

2. Information collected automatically

The app uses the following Google services, which collect some information from your device when you use Moola Flow:

Service What it collects Why
Firebase Crashlytics Crash logs, device model, operating system version, app state at the time of a crash and a Crashlytics installation ID Finding and fixing crashes
Google Analytics for Firebase App usage events (such as app opens and screens viewed), an app instance ID, device information and approximate location derived from your IP address Understanding how the app is used so it can be improved
Firebase Cloud Messaging A Firebase installation ID and app version Delivering notifications to your device
Firebase App Check A device integrity token from your platform's integrity service (Play Integrity on Android) Protecting our servers from abuse by confirming requests come from the genuine app
Google AdMob Advertising ID, IP address (which can indicate approximate location), ad interactions and diagnostic information Showing ads, measuring ad performance and preventing ad fraud

3. Information that stays on your device

  • App lock PIN: stored only on your device, as a salted hash in your device's encrypted storage. It is never sent to us.
  • Fingerprint and face unlock: handled entirely by Android or iOS. The app only learns whether the check passed and never receives your biometric data.
  • Reports: PDF and CSV reports are created on your device. They leave your device only if you choose to print, save or share them.
  • Settings and cache: your theme choice and a local copy of your data (so the app loads quickly) are stored on your device.

4. How we use information

  • To provide the app: saving and syncing your records, calculating balances, tracking budgets and building reports.
  • To create and manage your account, including verification and password reset emails.
  • To provide Premium and remove ads for subscribers.
  • To keep the app and your account secure and to prevent fraud and abuse.
  • To diagnose crashes and improve the app.
  • To show ads in the free version of the app.

We do not send marketing emails, and we do not use your money records for advertising.

Where the EU or UK General Data Protection Regulation applies, we rely on these legal bases: performing our agreement with you (providing the app), our legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention and fixing crashes) and your consent (personalised advertising where consent is required).

5. How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only in these cases:

  • Service providers: Google provides the services the app runs on, including Firebase (sign-in, database, file storage, crash reporting, analytics, messaging and App Check), Google Sign-In and Google Play Billing. Google processes this information on our behalf.
  • Advertising: Google AdMob receives the information described in section 2 to show and measure ads, and uses it under Google's own policies.
  • Legal reasons: if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users, the public or us.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy, Privacy and Security in Firebase and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

6. Advertising

The free version of Moola Flow shows ads from Google AdMob. Depending on where you live and the choices you make, ads may be personalised using your advertising ID.

  • In regions where consent is required (such as the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings, under Privacy, then Ads.
  • A Premium subscription removes ads from the app.

See how Google uses information for advertising.

7. Storage and security

  • Your account and records are stored on Google Cloud servers through Firebase. These servers may be located outside your country.
  • Data is encrypted in transit using TLS, and Google encrypts stored data at rest.
  • Database and file storage security rules allow only your signed-in account to read or change your records and photos.
  • You can add an app lock with a PIN or fingerprint and face unlock for extra protection on your device.

No method of storing or sending data is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

8. How long we keep information

  • Account and records: kept while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, records and photos are permanently deleted from our database and file storage straight away.
  • Crash reports: kept by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days.
  • Analytics data: kept by Google Analytics for no longer than 14 months.
  • Advertising data: kept by Google under its own retention policies.
  • Purchase records: Google Play keeps records of purchases under its own policies.

9. Your choices and rights

  • View and edit: you can view and change your records and profile in the app at any time.
  • Delete records: you can delete individual transactions and receipt photos in the app.
  • Delete your account: in the app go to Profile, then Delete account. If you no longer have the app, see how to request account deletion.
  • Export: you can export your monthly reports as PDF or CSV.
  • Ads: manage ad personalisation as described in section 6.

Depending on where you live, including under Zambia's Data Protection Act, 2021 and, where it applies, the GDPR, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. To make a request, email us at the address below. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority; in Zambia this is the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

10. Children

Moola Flow is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page. If the changes are significant, we will also let you know in the app or on our Google Play listing.
