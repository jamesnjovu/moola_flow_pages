Delete your Moola Flow account

You can delete your Moola Flow account and all of its data at any time, either in the app or by emailing us. Moola Flow is developed by James Njovu.

Option 1: Delete in the app

  1. Open Moola Flow and sign in.
  2. Tap the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Tap Delete to confirm.

Your account and data are deleted straight away, and you are signed out.

If you see "Your session has expired. Please sign in again.", sign out, sign back in, and repeat the steps. For your security, an account can only be deleted shortly after signing in.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app, or cannot sign in, email us from the email address you use for Moola Flow (if you sign in with Google, use that Google account's address).

Email a deletion request

Or write to njovujames@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Moola Flow account". We may reply to confirm the request comes from the account owner. We will delete your account and data within 30 days and let you know when it is done.

What is deleted

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. We delete:

  • Your sign-in account (email address, name and sign-in method)
  • Your profile, including your profile picture and currency and language preferences
  • All of your transactions, accounts, categories and budgets
  • All receipt photos you have attached
  • Your Premium status record

Deleting your account in the app also clears the app lock and local copy of your data from that device.

What may be kept, and for how long

Data Kept for
Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics), linked to an installation ID rather than your name or email Up to 90 days
App usage analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase) Up to 14 months
Advertising data collected by Google AdMob Under Google's retention policies
Records of Premium purchases held by Google Play Under Google Play's policies

Have a Premium subscription? Deleting your account does not cancel it. Cancel it in Google Play first: open the Play Store, tap your profile icon, then Payments & subscriptions, Subscriptions, Moola Flow and Cancel subscription. You can also go to play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions.

Delete some data without deleting your account

You can delete individual transactions and receipt photos in the app at any time. They are permanently removed from our servers.

For more about how we handle your data, read our privacy policy.
